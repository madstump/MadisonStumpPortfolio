Teaching philosophy

Students learn best when the work feels like something real people actually make.

I build lessons around visible outcomes: websites, pitch decks, game demos, design posters, coding puzzles, prototypes, portfolios, and reflection artifacts. My goal is to make technical skills feel approachable while still connecting them to industry expectations, communication, and creative problem-solving.
